How to Roll

📱 Hold your phone flat-ish like a fancy dinner tray. Tilt it and the ball rolls downhill. Newton approves.

🎯 Roll into the glowing green hole to win. Faster = more ★.

🕳️ Red holes are traps. Fall in and the ball respawns at the start, slightly embarrassed.

🎚️ Recalibrate (⊕) whenever you change how you're holding the phone. The angle you hold at countdown "GO" becomes "level."

Worlds:
1–10 Rookie Roll — gentle warm-up
11–20 Pit Panic — trap holes appear
21–30 Loop-de-Loop — mazes with shortcuts & loops
31–40 Ice Rink — the floor is slippery. Very.
41–50 Lights Out — you only see near the ball 🔦

🔒 Tip: Swipe into Control Center and turn on Portrait Orientation Lock so iOS doesn't spin the screen mid-tilt. The app also refuses to rotate on its own.

🖥️ No tilt sensor? Drag your finger on the screen like a joystick, or use arrow keys.
